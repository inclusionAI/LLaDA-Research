LLaDA-8B was trained from scratch as a masked diffusion language model. Its forward process masks tokens, while the learned reverse process reconstructs language through iterative denoising.

Official checkpoints are available as [LLaDA-8B-Base](https://huggingface.co/GSAI-ML/LLaDA-8B-Base) and [LLaDA-8B-Instruct](https://huggingface.co/GSAI-ML/LLaDA-8B-Instruct).
