LLaDA-MoE 7B-A1B was trained from scratch on about 20 trillion tokens, with 7B total parameters and roughly 1.4B active parameters. It is the earlier from-scratch sparse lineage, distinct from LLaDA2.0.

Official checkpoints are available as [Base](https://huggingface.co/inclusionAI/LLaDA-MoE-7B-A1B-Base) and [Instruct](https://huggingface.co/inclusionAI/LLaDA-MoE-7B-A1B-Instruct).
