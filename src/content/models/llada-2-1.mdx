LLaDA2.1 combines Mask-to-Token and Token-to-Token editing to accelerate diffusion generation. It supports S mode and Q mode across the 16B mini and 100B flash scales.

The release includes checkpoints at both scales.

Browse the [official collection](https://huggingface.co/collections/inclusionAI/llada21) for the complete release.
