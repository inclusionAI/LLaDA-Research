LLaDA 1.5 builds on LLaDA-8B-Instruct with variance-reduced preference optimization. VRPO lowers the variance of the ELBO preference gradient to improve math, code, instruction following, and alignment.

The release provides one official [LLaDA 1.5 checkpoint](https://huggingface.co/GSAI-ML/LLaDA-1.5).
