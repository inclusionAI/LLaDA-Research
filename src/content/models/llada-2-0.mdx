LLaDA2.0 spans the 16B mini and 100B flash MoE models, including CAP variants. It introduces a systematic autoregressive-to-diffusion conversion path for scaling diffusion language models.

This sparse family is distinct from the earlier from-scratch LLaDA-MoE lineage. Checkpoints include [mini](https://huggingface.co/inclusionAI/LLaDA2.0-mini), [flash](https://huggingface.co/inclusionAI/LLaDA2.0-flash), [mini-CAP](https://huggingface.co/inclusionAI/LLaDA2.0-mini-CAP), and [flash-CAP](https://huggingface.co/inclusionAI/LLaDA2.0-flash-CAP).
